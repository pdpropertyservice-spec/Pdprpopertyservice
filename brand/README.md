# Pav's Window Co. logo kit

Everything in `brand/logo/` is vector (SVG) with all lettering converted to outlines, so a
sign shop, printer, or embroiderer can open it without needing the fonts installed.
`brand/logo/png/` holds transparent PNG exports at 2000px wide for quick use.

## Files

| File | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `logo-horizontal-*` | Website header, business card front, vehicle doors, email signature |
| `logo-stacked-*` | Square spaces: social profile images, shirt chest, stickers |
| `mark-*` | Icon on its own: favicon, app tile, small embroidery, hat front |
| `badge-*` | Round seal with phone and service line: shirt backs, vehicle rear, decals |
| `icon-tile.svg` + `png/icon-tile-*.png` | Favicon and phone home-screen icon |

Each design comes in four tones:

- `night`: full color on dark backgrounds (silver P, emerald window). The primary version.
- `day`: full color on white or light backgrounds (ink P, emerald window).
- `mono-white`: one color, white. For dark vinyl, screen printing, embroidery on dark fabric.
- `mono-black`: one color, black. For stamps, faxes, engraving, light vinyl.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#070B09` | Dark background, card front, shirts |
| Ink | `#0E1A14` | P body on light backgrounds, body text |
| Emerald | `#0F9A66` | Window panes, "Window Co." on light backgrounds |
| Emerald bright | `#3EE39B` | Sparkle, "Window Co." on dark backgrounds |
| Emerald deep | `#075B3D` | Shadow end of the emerald gradient |
| Silver | `#C9D1D5` | Mid tone of the brushed P |

Vinyl and apparel vendors usually want a single Pantone or thread color: Emerald `#0F9A66`
is close to Pantone 3405 C, and the night background is close to Pantone Black 6 C.

## Fonts

- Wordmark: Sora ExtraBold (free, Google Fonts)
- "Window Co." and badge text: Inter Bold (free, Google Fonts)

## Rules of thumb

- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the "P" stem's width.
- Don't stretch, recolor, or add outlines. Use the mono versions when color isn't available.
- Minimum size: the horizontal logo should be at least 1 inch wide in print; the mark alone
  works down to 0.4 inch (and 16px on screen).

The kit is generated by a script, so edits to the geometry or text are easy to reapply
across every variant. Ask for the generator if you need a new format or a change.
