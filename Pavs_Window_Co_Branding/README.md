## Changes in this pass
**Premium**
- Gradients re-sampled from the original: every silver/green surface now uses colour stops measured from the reference (letters, roof slopes, blade, handle, head, rubber, bars, ribbons, WINDOW CO., slogan), plus a faint brushed-grain pattern and darker bevel grooves. Mean silver brightness is within about 5–10/255 of the original; forest green within about 5–9/255.
- Swoosh ribbons, squeegee head/handle/hole re-fitted to the original outline; plate regenerated with smoother, less lumpy edges; apostrophe redrawn as a clean conventional comma shape (round head, curved tapered tail); siding darkened.
- Lettering, proportions and transparent background unchanged.

**Simplified**: black outlines thinned (7 → 5 units keyline; plate border tightened), deeper green (`#1B7A3A` → `#166A35`), silver unchanged, same refined shapes and clean apostrophe. Tested on white, black, grey and transparent.

**Embroidery**: genuinely simplified – water drops, rivets, handle hole, siding, chimney, inner roof line and the small swoosh ribbons removed; roof is one solid silver band; squeegee is a single solid silhouette; apostrophe/tapered tips kept at stitchable widths; slogan removed. 3 solid colours, no gradients, strokes or disconnected specks (smallest piece 1.96 mm² at 3.5 in).

**Social**: new dedicated icon – the P monogram plus the green/silver swoosh (swoosh compressed for a compact square). Not the full logo shrunk. Tested at 512/256/128/64/48 px, square and circle-cropped.

## Remaining limitations
- Not an exact copy: the original’s photographic metal texture, glows and window scene are approximated (gradients, light grain, simple sky/pine window).
- PAV’S letters, swoosh and plate are fitted to the original (not hand-drawn from a spec), so tiny curve differences exist. WINDOW CO./slogan use Montserrat-style outlines fitted to your artwork.
- Embroidery: ~1–2% of the area (sharp corners, tapered ends) is narrower than 0.8 mm at 3.5 in; the digitizer will round these. Black counts as a thread colour. WINDOW CO. is about 4.6 mm cap height – readable but near the practical minimum.
- Icon: swoosh ribbons blur together below about 48 px; the P stays clear.
- Not tested in a cutter/RIP/digitizing software or a print proof, so I am not calling these print-ready. Get a proof or sew-out first.
